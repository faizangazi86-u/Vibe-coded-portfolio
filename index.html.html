About Me

I'm Mohammed Faizan Gazi, a Computer Science student at Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU). I come from a multi-business family background — growing up around entrepreneurship gave me a real-world understanding of commerce, customer behavior, and operations from an early age.

That business mindset is what drives my approach to data — I don't just build dashboards, I ask what decision does this enable? My work spans revenue analysis, sales performance, fraud detection, and marketing analytics — all told through clean, purposeful visuals.

"Analytics isn't just about charts — it's about telling stories that drive action."

Beyond data, I'm a Vibe Coder — I build with AI tools like Cursor, Lovable, and Framer to bring ideas to life fast. I believe the future belongs to those who can combine data intuition with AI-powered building.

I'm also a Student Organizer, Speaker, and Anchor — someone who leads teams, commands rooms, and thrives on challenges. Off the analytics desk, I serve as the Chief Executive of VTU KLB FC, leading operations, team management, and strategic decisions. Leadership is not a title, it's a habit.

Based in India · Open to remote & on-site opportunities.
